01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

People first.
Curious by nature.

My career started in the automotive world, where I learned that the best part of working with technology isn't the technology itself. It's seeing someone go from confused to confident.

At Mercedes-Benz Victoria, I worked across product education, sales, and customer support. Whether I was walking someone through connected features or helping solve a problem after a purchase, I liked being the person who could make things clearer.

That's what draws me to Customer Success: building relationships, understanding what someone actually needs, and helping them get value from the tools they're using.

Outside of that, I'm usually taking photos, editing a video, or tinkering with a new idea. I enjoy learning by building things, even when the first version is a little rough.

SIDE PROJECT · CONVERSATIONAL TECHNOLOGY

What if getting help from a business felt a little easier?

I built a customer-support chat prototype for a home-services business to explore that question. I organized the knowledge base, designed the conversation paths, and tested how it handled everyday questions, lead requests, and situations needing a real person.

It taught me a lot about how easily an automated assistant can sound confident when it shouldn't — and why clear boundaries matter.

Tested prototype, not a live customer deployment.
